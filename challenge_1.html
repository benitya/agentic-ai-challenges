CHALLENGE 01

Flexible Autonomy

The Flexible Autonomy Challenge

Evaluating how agents interleave human inputs with their outputs and justify their next steps.

What is this challenge?

The challenge explores how an AI agent and a human or evaluator can work together on a case, rather than treating the agent's first answer as the final answer.

A typical workflow allows the agent to make an initial decision, receive feedback, investigate further when needed, and return with a revised conclusion.

The central question

How can we evaluate whether an agent is using human input effectively while still completing a task efficiently and producing a well-justified outcome?

Typical workflow

From initial decision to convergence

1

Agent classifies

The agent proposes a decision such as advance, reject or flag, with confidence and an explanation.

2

Evaluator reviews

A human or evaluator reviews the case and may request further investigation.

3

Agent investigates

The agent gathers more information and returns a request for input or revised conclusion.

4

Repeat to agreement

The exchange continues until agreement is reached or a defined turn limit is reached.

What we may evaluate

Evaluation dimensions

Convergence qualityCorrectness and quality of the final outcome and explanation.
EfficiencyTurns, latency and cost required to reach the outcome.
Human factorsSatisfaction, workload and calibrated trust.
