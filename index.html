Why this programme

Benchmarks don't measure what deployment demands

Existing AI benchmarks say nothing about how well models perform with a human as partner, how to cut their energy use, or how they stay accountable once deployed. We focus on three objectives sought in real-world deployments across Public Services, Businesses, and the Public.

🤝

Human flourishing

Human–AI partnerships that ensure human flourishing, not just task throughput.

🌱

Frugal AI

Reducing the energy demands of AI while maintaining real performance levels.

⚖️

Accountability

Accountability in complex human–AI systems where many parties contribute.

Three international challenges

Turning Responsible AI principles into reproducible evidence

The focus is on real-world challenges, not artificial ones — using datasets and configurations that industry and government find most challenging.

01
Flexible Autonomy

The Flexible Autonomy Challenge

Build an LLM transcription, chat or voice agent that works with one or more humans — and measure task performance alongside human satisfaction and workload.

02
Frugal Agentic AI

The Frugal Agentic AI Challenge

Build a four-node Raspberry Pi 5 multi-agent system and test whether better coordination retains useful capability while cutting energy — strengthening sovereignty and resilience.

03
Accountability

The Accountability Challenge

For a complex human–agent system, produce verifiable explanations that let people identify who contributed to an outcome and who is responsible for errors.

Flexible Autonomy — typical workflow

1

Agent classifies

Proposes advance / reject / flag, with confidence and an explanation.

2

Evaluator reviews

Human or LLM-as-judge — may pass back "investigate X first."

3

Agent investigates

Gathers more, returns a request for input or a revised conclusion.

4

Repeat to agreement

Loops until agreement, or a turn cap is reached.

Because Challenge 3 consumes what 1 and 2 produce , the programme's three challenges compound rather than merely coexist.
