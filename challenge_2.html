Challenge 02
Frugal Agentic AI

The Frugal Agentic AI Challenge

Can a team of cooperating AI agents deliver useful answers while minimising energy consumption and communication on a small network of low-power computers?

Draft specification v0.1| Subject to review by the challenge chairs
Part A

Resource-efficient multi-agent systems

The challenge explores how to build agent systems that can deliver useful and reliable results while operating efficiently on a small network of low-power devices. The focus is on minimising energy consumption and communication overhead, without compromising output quality.

♧This challenge uses five low-power nodes (e.g. Raspberry Pi 5), with model weights selected by the organisers and an isolated network.

◉   The central question

How can AI agents complete useful tasks while minimising energy and communication on a small network of low-power computers?

Tasks

Two proposed tasks

01   Single query

Answer independent prompts, such as questions about passages, summaries and drafting tasks.

02   Multi-turn conversation

Handle a scripted conversation with evolving requirements and retain relevant context.

Task details, example prompts and constraints remain subject to review.

Submission requirements

What teams will submit

✓ Setup code for the orchestrator and four agents

✓ Connection self-test

✓ Orchestrator implementing the challenge API

✓ Configuration (roles, prompts, routing, memory)

✓ Method description (maximum 2 pages)

Model weights are provided by the organisers. Packaging, dependencies and size limits are TBD.

Evaluation

What will be measured (proposed)

Energy consumption

Total system energy, including nodes and switch.

Inter-agent communication

Messages delivered and data transferred.

Answer quality

Useful responses, with a proposed minimum threshold.

Response time and behaviour

Latency, idle power and token usage.

Scoring, thresholds and measurement settings need committee agreement.
